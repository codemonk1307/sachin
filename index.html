About

Engineer first, product-minded throughout.

I'm Sachin, a software engineer, writer, and someone who is endlessly curious about how things work, whether that's a distributed system or a line of Urdu poetry. I build things for a living and I build things because I can't help it.

I have 4+ years of professional experience shipping production systems, from AI-powered healthcare tools used by tens of thousands of practitioners to identity infrastructure and subscription platforms built to scale. Competitive programming sharpened how I think: top 3% in Google Code Jam, national finalist at Smart India Hackathon.

Outside of code, I run a literary collective called Ain Sheen Qaf: The Muted Void, write about love and solitude, host mushairas, and dream about building something like Jarvis someday. I care about craft: in engineering, in language, and in everything I put my name on.
